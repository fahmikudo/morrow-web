Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026 · Last updated 11 November 2026

Morrow (“Morrow”, “we”, “us”) is a personal productivity application at https://mrrw.my.id. This policy explains what information the service collects, why we collect it, how it is stored and shared, and the choices available to you. It applies to the Morrow web application, the Morrow API, and the integrations and notifications described below.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you register we collect your name, email address, and password. Passwords are stored only as salted Argon2id hashes and are never returned by the API. Your profile may also contain an optional role and time zone. Session tokens are stored by your browser in local storage; the server stores only a hash of each token.

Content you create

Morrow stores the workspace content you enter or import: tasks and their descriptions, labels, time boxes, and external links; projects; areas; notes and their rich text; inbox items; calendar events; habits and habit check-ins; and related settings. This content is associated with your account and is not public.

Attachments

If attachments are enabled and you upload a file to a project, task, or note, the file is stored in an S3-compatible object store (Cloudflare R2 when configured) and its metadata (name, size, content type, and owner) is stored in our database. The API issues short-lived signed URLs for upload and download; it does not proxy file bytes.

Google Calendar (optional)

If you connect Google Calendar, you authorize Morrow through Google OAuth. We request https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.calendarlist.readonly. We use these scopes to list your calendars, read events from the calendar you select, store copies of those events in Morrow, and create, update, or delete events in that Google calendar when you create, edit, or delete the corresponding event in Morrow. OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-GCM. You can disconnect the integration at any time in Settings, which also removes the stored tokens.

AI assistant (optional)

The assistant is off until you enable it and save a provider API key. When enabled, Morrow sends a bounded JSON snapshot of your workspace to DeepSeek to generate the reply. That snapshot includes tasks, projects, notes (including note text), inbox items, events, habits, areas, and the user request, together with identifiers needed to cite sources. Your DeepSeek API key is stored encrypted and is never returned by the API. If you do not enable the assistant, no workspace content is sent to DeepSeek. Replies generated locally in the browser without the assistant remain on your device.

Pepy task sync (optional, feature-gated)

Accounts explicitly allowed to use the SoftwareSeni Pepy integration can push opted-in, time-boxed tasks and completion times to Pepy. The integration sends the task fields needed for that sync (title, key, description as configured, dates, times, status, and completion time) to the Pepy API. It is disabled unless the account is enabled for it.

Push notifications

If you enable browser notifications, your browser creates a Web Push subscription. We store the push endpoint, its public encryption keys (p256dh and auth), the subscription time, and an optional user-agent string so reminders can be delivered to your devices. Reminders are derived from due tasks, upcoming events, and scheduled habits. You can disable notifications in Settings or in your browser.

Technical and security data

Our servers log request metadata such as method, path, status code, response size, duration, request ID, and the authenticated user ID when applicable. Web server and CDN logs may also include IP address and user-agent for security, abuse prevention, and debugging. We do not log request bodies. Log files are rotated and kept for a limited period (currently up to 14 days).

2. How we use information

  • Provide, operate, and secure the Morrow application and your account.
  • Store and display your workspace content across devices.
  • Run reminder scheduling and, if enabled, deliver Web Push notifications.
  • Sync calendars and tasks when you enable those integrations.
  • Generate assistant replies when you enable the assistant.
  • Diagnose failures, prevent abuse, and maintain service reliability.
  • Respond to support, privacy, and data-deletion requests.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers in the application. We do not use your content to train our own models.

3. Google user data

Morrow accesses Google user data only after you explicitly connect Google Calendar, and only for the scopes and purposes described above. We use Google user data solely to provide the calendar sync you request: displaying your events in Morrow and mirroring your Morrow event changes to the calendar you selected.

  • We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
  • We do not allow humans to read Google user data unless you ask us to for support, it is necessary for security or legal compliance, or the data is aggregated and de-identified.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as needed to provide the feature, comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice.
  • If the AI assistant is enabled, event data in your workspace — including events synced from Google Calendar — is included in the context sent to DeepSeek as described in section 1. Disable the assistant if you do not want this processing.
Morrow’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke Morrow’s access at any time from your Google Account permissions page or by disconnecting Google Calendar in Morrow Settings. Revocation stops future access; previously synced copies remain in your workspace until you delete them or your account.

5. Cookies and local storage

Morrow does not use advertising or analytics cookies. The web app stores your session token and a read-only snapshot cache in browser local storage, and a service worker caches application assets so the app can load reliably. Cloudflare may set security cookies (for example, bot management) as part of protecting the site. You can clear browser storage at any time; doing so signs you out and removes cached data from that device.

6. Retention

Account and workspace data are retained while your account is active. When you delete content, it is removed from the active database; related records are removed by database cascades. Account deletion removes your profile, content, subscriptions, tokens, and integration credentials. Security and access logs are kept for a limited period (currently up to 14 days). Encrypted backups, where used, may retain data for a short additional period before rotation.

7. Security

Traffic is served over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with Argon2id. Session tokens are stored hashed server-side. Google OAuth tokens, provider API keys, and other integration secrets are encrypted at rest with AES-GCM. Access to the production database and secrets is limited to the operator. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access and copy: request a copy of the account data and workspace content associated with your account.
  • Correction: update your profile and content in the app, or ask us to correct inaccurate account data.
  • Deletion: delete individual items in the app, or request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent: disconnect Google Calendar, disable the assistant, disable push notifications, or stop using the Pepy integration at any time.
  • Object or restrict: contact us if you believe processing is unlawful or excessive.

To exercise these rights, email privacy@mrrw.my.id. We may need to verify that the request comes from the account owner. We aim to respond within 30 days. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

9. Children

Morrow is a productivity tool for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

Morrow and the providers listed above may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards offered by those providers, such as standard contractual clauses.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the service or legal requirements. The “Last updated” date at the top will change, and material changes will be announced in the app. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: privacy@mrrw.my.id.
